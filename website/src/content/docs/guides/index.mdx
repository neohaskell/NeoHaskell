Choose the stage of your application that needs attention. Each guide begins with
a familiar problem and develops the implementation and evidence you need.

- [Build an application](/build/): commands, state, queries, permissions, and tests.
- [Connect external systems](/connect/): external services, files, email, and AI features.
- [Operate and evolve](/operate/): persistence, deployment, diagnosis, and recovery.
- [Deployment probe reference](/guides/deployment/): the existing probe and rollout contract.
- [Capability reference](/reference/capabilities/): jump to one specific topic.

The recurring ecommerce practice project illustrates these techniques. Choose the guides relevant to your own application and use the examples to practise.

If you are evaluating the approach, start with [fit and tradeoffs](/start/fit-and-tradeoffs/).
